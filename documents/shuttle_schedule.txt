SUNSHINE CONDOMINIUM ASSOCIATION
SHUTTLE SCHEDULE

SERVICE AREA
Our complimentary shuttle service connects residents to key locations in the area.

WEEKDAY SCHEDULE (Monday - Friday)

Route 1: Shopping District
- Departs Sunshine Condo: 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 6:00 PM
- Stops: Mall Plaza, Whole Foods Market, Target Center
- Return times: 10:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM, 7:00 PM

Route 2: Medical Center
- Departs Sunshine Condo: 8:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM
- Stops: City Hospital, Medical Plaza, Pharmacy Center
- Return times: 9:30 AM, 12:30 PM, 3:30 PM

Route 3: Downtown/Transit Hub
- Departs Sunshine Condo: 7:00 AM, 8:30 AM, 5:00 PM, 6:30 PM
- Direct service to Metro Station and Downtown Business District
- Return times: 8:00 AM, 9:30 AM, 6:00 PM, 7:30 PM

WEEKEND SCHEDULE (Saturday - Sunday)

Route 1: Shopping District
- Departs Sunshine Condo: 10:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM
- Return times: 11:30 AM, 2:30 PM, 5:30 PM

Route 2: Entertainment District
- Departs Sunshine Condo: 11:00 AM, 5:00 PM, 8:00 PM
- Stops: Movie Theater, Restaurant Row, Arts Center
- Return times: 1:00 PM, 7:00 PM, 10:00 PM

HOLIDAY SCHEDULE
- Shuttle operates on reduced weekend schedule on major holidays
- No service on: New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day

RESERVATIONS
- Shuttle operates on first-come, first-served basis
- Capacity: 12 passengers
- For large groups (6+), please call ahead: (555) 123-RIDE
- Reservation line open: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM daily

ACCESSIBILITY
- All shuttles are wheelchair accessible
- Please notify driver if assistance is needed
- Service animals welcome

POLICIES
- Must show resident ID or guest pass
- No smoking or vaping
- No food or open beverages
- Be ready 5 minutes before scheduled departure
- Shuttle will not wait more than 3 minutes past scheduled time

Shuttle Coordinator: shuttle@sunshinecondo.com
Phone: (555) 123-RIDE (7433)
Emergency: Contact main office at (555) 123-4567
