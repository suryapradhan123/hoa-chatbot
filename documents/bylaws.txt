CONDOMINIUM ASSOCIATION BYLAWS

Article I: Name and Purpose
The name of this organization shall be "Sunshine Condominium Association" (hereinafter referred to as "the Association"). The purpose of the Association is to manage, maintain, and preserve the common areas and facilities of the Sunshine Condominium complex.

Article II: Membership
Section 1: All owners of units within the Sunshine Condominium complex shall be members of the Association.
Section 2: Membership is automatic and mandatory upon purchase of a unit.
Section 3: Each unit is entitled to one vote in Association matters.

Article III: Board of Directors
Section 1: The Board shall consist of five (5) directors elected by the membership.
Section 2: Directors shall serve two-year staggered terms.
Section 3: The Board shall meet monthly to conduct Association business.

Article IV: Meetings
Section 1: Annual Meeting - The Association shall hold an annual meeting in January of each year.
Section 2: Special Meetings - Special meetings may be called by the Board President or by petition of 20% of the membership.
Section 3: Quorum - A quorum for meetings shall be 30% of the membership.

Article V: Assessments
Section 1: Monthly assessments are due on the first day of each month.
Section 2: Late fees of $50 will be charged for payments received after the 10th of the month.
Section 3: The Board may levy special assessments for major repairs or improvements with approval of 60% of the membership.

Article VI: Common Areas
Section 1: Common areas include hallways, elevators, parking areas, pool, fitness center, and landscaped areas.
Section 2: All owners have equal rights to use common areas subject to rules and regulations.

Article VII: Amendments
These bylaws may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the membership at any annual or special meeting, provided notice of the proposed amendment was given at least 30 days in advance.

Adopted: January 15, 2020
Last Revised: March 10, 2024
