SUNSHINE CONDOMINIUM ASSOCIATION
RULES AND REGULATIONS

1. NOISE AND QUIET HOURS
   - Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM on weekdays
   - Quiet hours are from 11:00 PM to 9:00 AM on weekends
   - No loud music, construction work, or other disturbing activities during quiet hours
   - Residents are responsible for noise from guests and pets

2. PARKING REGULATIONS
   - Each unit is assigned one parking space
   - Guest parking is available in designated areas on a first-come, first-served basis
   - No parking in fire lanes or blocking driveways
   - Vehicles must display valid parking permits
   - Abandoned vehicles will be towed at owner's expense after 72 hours notice

3. PET POLICY
   - Maximum of two pets per unit
   - Dogs must be on leash in common areas
   - Owners must clean up after pets immediately
   - Aggressive or dangerous pets are prohibited
   - Pet weight limit: 50 pounds maximum per dog

4. POOL AND AMENITIES
   - Pool hours: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily
   - Children under 14 must be supervised by an adult
   - No glass containers in pool area
   - Fitness center access: 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM
   - Reserve the clubhouse 48 hours in advance for events

5. GARBAGE AND RECYCLING
   - Trash chutes available on each floor
   - Recycling bins located in parking garage
   - Bulk items must be scheduled for pickup
   - No dumping of furniture or large items in trash areas

6. BALCONIES AND PATIOS
   - No storage of flammable materials
   - BBQ grills must be electric or gas (no charcoal)
   - No hanging laundry visible from outside
   - Plants and decorations must be secure

7. ALTERATIONS AND IMPROVEMENTS
   - All modifications to units must be approved by the Board
   - Submit written requests 30 days before starting work
   - Contractors must provide proof of insurance
   - Work hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday

8. MOVE-IN/MOVE-OUT PROCEDURES
   - Reserve elevator 48 hours in advance
   - Provide $500 refundable deposit for potential damages
   - Use protective padding on elevator walls
   - Complete move within reserved time slot

9. SECURITY AND ACCESS
   - Do not prop open security doors
   - Report suspicious activity to management immediately
   - Do not share access codes or keys with non-residents
   - Lost keys/fobs must be reported within 24 hours

10. GUEST POLICY
    - Guests may stay maximum 14 consecutive days
    - Register long-term guests with management office
    - Residents are responsible for guest behavior

Violations of these rules may result in fines and/or legal action.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
